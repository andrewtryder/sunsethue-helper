## 2026-06-22 - Icon-only buttons accessibility\n**Learning:** The frontend uses several icon-only buttons (search, edit, delete) which lacked ARIA labels, making them inaccessible to screen readers. We need to ensure all icon-only buttons have `aria-label` attributes and their decorative child elements have `aria-hidden="true"`.\n**Action:** Add `aria-label` to any new icon-only buttons and `aria-hidden="true"` to inner icons across the codebase.
